aMjanesutano maha adbhutanO
taraNi maMDalake muTTi haarida
balada shrutiyo vikrama gatiyo
suraraayudha neggisida

mUrlOka guruvO kalpataruvO || 1 ||

raamapadaaMbuja biMbaparaaga 
madhupano dEvaadhipano
taamasa raavaNanedegottida
I karavo siDilabbaravO
prEmadi raNadoLag~haari
rathavanodda maarutiyo puNya mUrutiyo
rOmake silkada hanumana
baalada saraLo daityariguruLo || 2 ||
