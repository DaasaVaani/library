```source-latin
ninna daruSanake baMdavanallavO
puNyavaMtara divya caraNa nODali baMde ||pa||

ellelli ninna vyaaptatanaviralikke
illige baruva kaaraNavaavudO
sollige kaMbhadali tOrida mahaamahima
alli illEnayya balla bhajakarige ||1||

karedaagalE ODi baMdodaguva svaami
maraLi gaavuda eNisi baralyaatakO
nere naMbidavarige AvallaadarEnu
aridavara manadoLage niMdaaDuva caMdava ||2||

kaThinavO ninna bhakutarana nODuva laabha
saTeyalla vEdagaLu saarutivakO
vaTu modalaada vaiShNavaagrEsaraMGri
triTiyaadarU enage sOkalu gatige daari ||3||

dhyaanakke silukuvanE ninna kaaNalibahudu
j~jaanigaLu eMtu baruvarO allige
anaMta janumadalli japa tapa hOma vrata
EnEnu maaDidarU iShTu jana kUDuvadE ||4||

nInidda sthaanadali sakala puNyakShEtra
nInidda sthaanadali sarvatIrtha
nInidda sthaanadali samasta taatvikaru
naanitta baruvudu ninage tiLiyadE swaami ||5||

idane laalisu jIyaa ninnadoMdE mUrti
nidaruSanavallade migilaavudo
padOpadige madhvamata hoMdida sujanara
hRudayadoLagaanaMtapari ninna rUpaMgaLu   ||6||

bhaLire tirumalaraaya ninna karuNaa rasake
nelegaaNenele serehoreyoLu
volidu bhakutarigaagi maduve hammikoMDe
sulabha dEvaradEva vijayaviThThala veMkaTaa ||7||
```
