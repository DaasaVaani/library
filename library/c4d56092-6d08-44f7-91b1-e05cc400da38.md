gaMgaadhara smara tripura haradEva ||pa.||
aMgajahara bhasitaaMga mahaaliMga
tuMgamahima mRugaaMkamouLi shiva ||a.pa.||

shraddhaa mUruti bhUtEsha sadyOjaata
pradyumna suteya suta matiya pradaata
sadamala mUruti hRRudaya sadanadoLu
vidhipitanaMghriya smaraNeya karuNisu ||1||

pramatha shrEShThara saMsEvita sumanasara prIta
haimavatiya prIta maaramaNage ati bhakuta
nI manObhimaaniyE prakhyaata
taamasaroLu tamasaadhana maaDisi
nI mOhipa dEvashUli kapaali ||2||

shuka dUrvaasa svarUpa jaigIsha rUpa
vaikaarikaadi trairUpa
lOkanaatha shrIveMkaTESana 
hRutkamaladi nalisuva kaalanEtra shiva ||3||
