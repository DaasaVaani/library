SaraNu SaraNu SaraNu dEvaadigaLvaMdita-
nahudO svaami gaNanaatha sharaNu ||pa||

akhiLa bhuvanadoLu pUjita
bhakuta janake nI saakShaata 
sakala vidyaavaradaata 
Sakutanahudayya prakhyaata ||1||

Suddha buddhara sahakaara
buddhi Iva Gana udaara
riddhigyaagIhya nI Adhaara 
siddhidaayaka viGnahara ||2||

janake maaDuve dOSha naaSa
anudinavu mati prakaaSa
dInamahipatiya manObhaavapUrita 
gaNaadhISa SaraNu sharaNu ||3||
