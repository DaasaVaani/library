AMjanEyane paaliso ninna shrIpaadakaMja manadalli tOriso ||pa||

saMjIvadharanE prabhaMjana tanaya dhanaMjaya kEtana maMjuLa mUruti || a.pa. ||

raamapaadaaMbuja bhRuMgaa sakala sadguNa dhaamaa
sajjanaaMtaraMgaa bhUmisuteya pariNaama tiLidu Araama 
muridu KaLastOma bhaMjisida nissIma baladhaama ripu bhIma Rujusaarvabhouma || 1 ||

lakShmaNa praaNaadhaaraa dashakaMdara vakShamardana ati shUraa akShaaraatiye
rakShOnikaradhyakShara maha mada shikShaNeyu kRuta dIkShaa 
balu dakSha rudhiraakSha koDu ninna parOkSha || 2 ||

udita bhaaskara prabhaava achyutaanaMta sadana sadaya svabhaava
tridashara guru gOpaalaviThalana pada bhajakaroLage modaliga nInE pavana 
maadhavana guNastavana maaDisu ninnavana || 3 ||
