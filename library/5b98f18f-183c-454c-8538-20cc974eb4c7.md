taa honnu taa gubbi taa honnu taa ||pa||

aagamavanu taMdu jagakitta kaige
saagarava mathisi sudhe taMda kaige
tUgi mattaaDuva sthUla kaayana kaige
saagara pati namma narasiMhana kaige ||1||

baliya daanava bEDi baMdaMtha kaige
ChaladiMda kShatriyara koMdaMtha kaige
kali vibhIShaNagabhayavittaMtha kaige
balu beTTa beraLalli hottaMtha kaige ||2||

pativrateyara vratavaLidaMtha kaige
hitavaajiyanEri meredaMtha kaige
sati shirOmaNi lakShmikaaMtana kaige
chatura heLavanakaTTeraMgana kaige ||3||
