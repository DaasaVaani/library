aLadirO summaniramma nI

hadinaalku jagava jOLiyalliTTa bavva
gOLiTTisutide bavva

mogavaritu dUradi niMtippa bavva ||2||

mogavaidu paNegaNNa bavva 
pulidogalu keMjeDe ruMDamaaleya bavva
bageyiMda peNNetti bavva 
taasogasile raamaraamenutide bavva  ||3||

maiyella kaNNina bavva 
tannakaiyalli vajrava bIsuva bavva
uyyalaaDutalide bavva ninna 
oyvenenuta baMtu halakaala bavva  ||4||

oMdalla nUralla bavva ninna
maMdira suttutavanaMta bavva
