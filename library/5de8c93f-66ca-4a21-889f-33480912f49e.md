raama eMbuva eraDu akSharada mahimeyanu
paamararu taavEnu ballarayya ||pa||

raa eMda maatradoLu rakta maaMsadoLidda
Ayasthigatavaada atipaapavannu
maayavanu maaDi mahaaraaya muktiya koDuva
daayavanu vaalmIki muniraaya balla ||1||

matte ma eMdenalu horabidda paapagaLu
otti oLage pOgadaMte kavaaTavaagi
citta kaayagaLa pavitra maaDuva pariya
bhaktavara hanumaMtanobba taa balla ||2||

dhareyoLInaamakke sarimigilu illeMdu
parama vEdagaLella pogaLutihavu
siriyarasa puraMdara viThalana naamavanu
shivakaashiyoLagippa shivanu taa balla ||3||
