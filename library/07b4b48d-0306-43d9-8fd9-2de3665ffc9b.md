elli shrItuLasiya vanavu

kaMgoLisuva maNikarNikeyu
tuMgabhadre kRuShNavENi tIrthagaLella

dira sUrya modalaadavaru
siriramaNana aaj~jeyali agaladaMte

RugvEda yajurvEda saama atharvaNa
aggaLisida vEdaghOShagaLu
agrabhaagadalive beTTadoDeyanalli
shIghradi oliva shrIpuraMdaraviThala ||3||
