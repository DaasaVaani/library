SObhaana SObhaanave ||pa||
SrIbhUarasa vEMkaTaraayage  ||apa||

aMdu kShIraaMbudhi mathisalaagi
iMdire haruShadiMdusi baMdu
kaMdarpakOTi laavaNyamUrutiyaada
maMdaaramaaleya haakida dEvage ||1||

janakana maneyalli raajaadhiraajaru
eNikeyilladale barutiralu
sanakaadivaMdyana kaMDu saMtOShadi
jaanaki maaleya haakida raamage ||2||

rukumanu SiSupaalaganujeyIyuveneMdu
sakala raayaralli niMdiralaagi
bhakutavatsalanannu kaMDu saMtOShadi 
rukumiNi maaleya haakida kRuShNage ||3||

satyabhaameyu nIle bhadre kaaLiMdiyu
mitraviMdeyu lakShaNeyu jaaMbavati
matte A sOLa saasira kannikeyara
pratyakShavaaLida kalyaaNanige ||4||

padumadESadalobba dEvaaMganeyu
padumamukhi SrutismRutiyu
padumanaabha SrI puraMdaraviThalage
padumaavatIpriya SrInivaasage ||5||
