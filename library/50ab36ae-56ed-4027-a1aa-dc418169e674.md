bhUShaNake bhUShaNa idu bhUShaNa
shEShagiri vaasa shrIvara veMkaTESa ||pa||

naaligege bhUShaNa naaraayaNa naama 
kaalige bhUShaNa hariyaatreyu
Alayake bhUShaNa tulasi vRuMdaavana vi-
shaala karNake bhUShaNa viShNu katheyu ||1||

daanavE bhUShaNa iruva hastaMgaLige
maanavE bhUShaNa maanavarige
j~jaanavE bhUShaNa muniyOgivararige
maaninige bhUShaNa patibhaktiyu ||2||

raMgananu nODuvudE kaMgaLige bhUShaNa
maMgaLaaMgage maNiva shira bhUShaNa
shRuMgaara tulasi maNi koraLige bhUShaNa
raMgaviThala nimma naama ati bhUShaNa ||3||
