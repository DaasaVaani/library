```source-latin
udara vairaagyavidu namma
padumanaabhanali lESa bhakutiyilla  ||pa||

udayakaaladoLeddu gaDagaDa naDuguta
nadiyali miMdeneMdu higgutali
mada matsara krOdha oLage tuMbiTTukoMDu
badiyalliddavarigaaScarya tOruvudu ||1||

kaMcugaarana biDaaradaMdadi
kaMcu hittaaLe pratimegaLa nerahi
miMcabEkeMdu balu jyOtigaLanu hacci
vaMcaneyiMdali pUje maaDuvudu ||2||

karadali japamaNi baayali maMtravu
ariveya musukanu mOrege haaki
parasatiyara guNa manadali smarisuta
parama vairaagyaSaali eMdenisuvudu ||3||

bUTakatanadali bahaLa bhakuti maaDi
diTanIta sariyaarillenisi
naaTaka strIyaMte bayala DaMbhava tOri
UTada maargada j~jaanavidallade ||4||

naanu eMbuda biTTu j~jaanigaLoDanaaDi
Enaadaru hari prEraNeyeMdu
dhyaanisi maunadi puraMdaraviThalana
kaaNade maaDida kaaryagaLellavu ||5||
```
