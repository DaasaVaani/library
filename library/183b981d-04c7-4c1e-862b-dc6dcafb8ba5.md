shreesha koLalanUdidanaMdu shrIdharaniMdu
vaasava vaMdita vaataja sEvita
vaasukishayananu vaare sunOTadi ||pa||

beraLa saMdili muraLi piDidu, muraari taanu
haruShadiMdali swaragaLa nuDidu, vaarijanEtra
araLu mallige saragaLa muDidu
maruLu maaDuta maDadiyellara ||1||

gouri gaaMdhaari gouLapaMtu gourIsha bhUShaNa
shourI saaraMga mOhanaviMtu saavEri suruTi
bhairavi byaagaDe Uduta niMtu
veera shrI kRuShNa vidha vidha raagadi ||2||

naarada tuMbura naaTyavanaaDe naLinanaabhana
girijaapatiyu vaMdisi bEDe gOpaalakRuShNana
varaguru vaMdita vijayaviThalarAya
haruSha paDisuta vaniteyarellara ||3||
