bhImaraayana naMbi bhImasEnana
bhImabhavavaaridhiya bEga dATuvare namma || pa ||

baka modalaada hiDiMbaka durjanara koMdu
suKapaDisidanaMdu dvijakulajaraaga kUDa || 1 ||

jaraasaMdhana kUDe vara yuddhavannE maaDi
saraku maaDade koMda dhaaruNiyoLage niMda || 2 ||

asura kIcakaneMbuva satiya kRuShNeya
baadhise GaasimaaDalu vaMdyara hEsade mOsadi koMda || 3 ||

kaDutRuShege dushyaasananoDala bagidu rakta
kuDidaMte tOrda nammoDeya droupadipriya || 4 ||

bhIma tanna marehokka sujanara kaava
svaami shrIhayavadana raamanaaNe satyavidu || 5 ||
