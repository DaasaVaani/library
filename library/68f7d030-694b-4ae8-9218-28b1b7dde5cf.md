```source-latin
SrIpatiyu namage saMpadavIyali vaa
NIpatiyu namage dIrGaayu koDali ||pa||

vara vibhudaranu poreye viShava kaMThadaliTTa
haranitya namage sahaaya maaDali
nararoLunnatavaada nitya bhOgaMgaLanu
puruhUta pUrNa maaDisali namage ||1||

vinuta siddhiprada viGnESanu dayadiMda
neneda kaaryagaLella neravErali
dina dinadi dhanvaMtri ApattugaLa kaLedu
mana haruShavittu mannisali biDade ||2||

niruta suj~jaanavanu Iva guru madhvaraaya
gurugaLaaSIrvaada namagaagali
puraMdara viThalana karuNeyiMdali nitya
surarolume namage susthiravaagali ||3||
```
