yOgya vaiShNava kulada BAgavatanallade||a.pa|| 

caturanenisi lakSha manemane tirugide | 
caturAnanA mAtu anusariside | 
satu citu AnaMda Atma harisa | 
rvOttamana dAsaradAsa eMbuvanallade ||1|| 

tAratamya paMcaBEdavane tiLidu SrI | 
mArutana matagaLE hAraisuta | 
karma vaMcaneyilladanudinadi mADuta | 
carisuta saMcitagAmi kaLevavanallade ||2|| 

patitapAvana SrIhari gurugaLanu mOdadi | 
kShitiyoLage Bajaneya dhanavennuta | 
atiSayadi SEShAdri hayavadananna nenenenedu | 
prItiyiMdali Bajisuvavanallade ||3||
