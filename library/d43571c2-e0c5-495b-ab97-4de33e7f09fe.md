kaavanayyana kaLuhu ramaNi ||pa||

kOvidaru paDeda baaDadaadikESavana || a.pa.||

caMdravaMSada raayananujasakhanaadavana
caMdravairiya mEle pavaDisidana
caMdramana sOdariya kai piDidaMthavana
caMdradhaamana manege kaLuhe kamalaakShi ||1||

kamalanaabhana kamalasaKakOTitEjana
kamalakOrakadi janisidanayyana
kamalavannu karadalli piDidihana bEgadali
kamalavadanana manege kaLuhe kamalaakShi ||2||

baalatanadali bahaLa asuraranu sILidana
lIleyiMdali bhaktaranu salahutihana
nIlamEGaSyaama bhaagyapuradali niMda
SrIlataaMgiyaramaNa AdikESavana ||3||
