```source-latin
Enu sukRutava mADidaLo yaSOde 
SrInidhiyAda kRuShNanna karedu muddisuvaLaMte  ||pa|| 

gaMgAjanakanige gaDige nIrerevaLaMte 
maMgaLAMgage SRuMgarisuvaLaMte  
tuMga BUdharanna toTTiloLu tUguvaLaMte 
kaMgaLigagOcarana karedappikoMbaLaMte ||1||

nagavanettidavana maguveMdettuvaLaMte
nigamagOcarana tA niTTipaLaMte 
agaNitasatvanna haggadi kaTTuvaLaMte 
mige nityatRuptage pAlanerevaLaMte ||2||

bahumuKanige BAme muddukoDuvaLaMte 
ahitalpanige hAvatuLidI yeMbaLaMte 
mahA daityadallaNanige bahu Bayava tOruvaLaMte 
mahima narasiMhage gummana tOruvaLaMte ||3|| 

caturmuKapitana sutaneMdettuvaLaMte 
Srutivinutage jOguLa pADuvaLaMte  
Sataravi tEjagAratiyanettuvaLaMte 
gataBItanige BAme rakSheyikkuvaLaMte ||4|| 

kaDegOla nENa kaiyali piDidolaviMda  
paDugaDala taDiya dvArake nilaya 
biDade nelesida hayavadana muddu 
uDupina kRuShNana uDiyoLettuvaLaMte ||5||
```
