kaTiyalli karaviTTanu | paMDharIrAya ||pa||
 
rAjasUya yAgadalli rAjESvara |
rAjaru modalAda surarellaru ||
BOjanavannE mADideMjalu modalAddu 
rAjIvAkShanu ettidAyAsadiMdalo ||1||

golla baalakaroDagUDi taa baMdu
gollatEramane pokku beNNe tiMdu
ballida tRuNaavarta modalaadasurarannu
mellane koMdaayaasadiMdalO ||2||

dhaaruNiyanu taMda danuja hiraNyakana
kOredaDeyiMda sILidariMdalo
vaarijamuKi sarvakaaladali niMtu
sEri surata maaDidaayaasadiMdalO ||3||

surapa tanayage sArathyavane mADi |
BaradiMda cakrava piDidudariMdalO |
paripari vidhadiMda kuduregaLane toLedu |
paripari kelasadi AyAsadiMdalo ||4||
 
mudadiMda vrajava hadinaaru saavira vaniteyaraa-
Lida madadiMdalo madagaja gamanEra 
madadaMta kRuShNa odagi maavana 
koMda AyaasadiMdalo ||5||

mamateyiMdali Bakta janaru tamma charaNa |
kamalayugavannu smarisuttaliralu ||
mamateyiMdali avara Bavava kaLeveneMdu |
kamalanABa SrI puraMdara viThala tA ||6||
