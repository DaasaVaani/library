Iga mADelO rAma dhyAnava nI |
mUganAgirabEDa ele mAnava ||pa||
 
kAlana dUtaru karevAga nI 
kAlageTTu kaNNu biDuvAga ||
nAlige seLakoMDu j~jAnageTTiruvAga 
nIlavarNana dhyAna odaguvudEnayyA ||1||
 
satisutareMba saMdaNiyoLu nI 
mati BraShTanAgi manadoLu ||
satata SrIlakShmI patiya nenedare sa-
dgati sanmArga pAraMpadavIvanayyA ||2||
 
yamana dUtaru baMdu eLevAga nI 
navevutta hottu kaLevAga ||
samadarSi puraMdara viThalana nAmavaa 
samayakke odagi tA baruvudEnayyA ||3||
