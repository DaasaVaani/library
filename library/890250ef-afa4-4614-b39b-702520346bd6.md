balu ramyavaagide SrIhariya maMca ||pa||
yalaruNi kularaaja raajESvarana maMca || apa||

pavanatanayanenipa paavanatara maMca
bhuvanatrayava potta bhaari maMca
kivigaLillada maMca SrInikEtana maMca
SivarUpadali SrIhariya olisida maMca ||1||

nIlaaMbaravanuTTu naLanaLisuva maMca
naalige eraDuLLa naija maMca
naalvattu kalpadali tapava maaDida maMca
taala musala halava piDidiha maMca ||2||

raamananujanaagi raNava jayisida maMca
taamasa rudrana paDeda maMca
bhImananujanoLu AvESisida maMca
jImUta mallaranu kuTTida maMca ||3||

jIvanaamakanaagi vyaaptanaada hariya
sEvisi sukhisuva divya maMca
saasira mukhadiMda stutisi higguva maMca
dEvakiya jaTharadali janisida maMca ||4||

vaaruNidEvige varanenisida maMca
saaruva bhaktaranu poreva maMca
kaaruNyanidhi jagannaathaviThalana vi-
haarakke yOgyavaada SESha maMca ||5||
