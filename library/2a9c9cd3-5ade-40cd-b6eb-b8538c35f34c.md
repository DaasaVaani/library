Enu kaaraNa matiyilla manave  ||pa||
hInaayavaaDadiru parara guNagaLa eNisi ||apa||

avana goDaveyEke avanaaru nInaaru
avana paapakke nI bhaagaadiye
avanuttamanaadaDavane badukida kaaNo
ava puNyamaaDidare avana kula uddhaara ||1||

ninage koDuvudaroLage bEDa baMdavanalla
ninage avanige Enu saMbaMdhavo
mane oDave vastugaLa haMcikoMbuvudalla
kShaNa kShaNake klESavanu maaDikoMbudu salla ||2||

ninna gati maargakke avanaDDa baahane
ninniMda paralOka avaniguMTe
ninnoLage nI tiLidu siri vijayaviThThalanna
sannutisi bhavarOga bannavanu kaLeyo ||3||
