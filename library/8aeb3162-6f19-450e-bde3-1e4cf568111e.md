vaasudEvana caraNakamala vaMdakane
sanyaasa kularatuna guruvyaasamuniraaya ||pa||

vaadigajasiMha durvaadi mRuga bhEruMDa
vaadiphaNigaruDa tattvaadi racita
vaadibhayaMkarane paravaadi kOlaahalane
vaadimastaka SUla madhura guNaSIla ||1||

vaiShNavOttama sadvaiShNava vrajaanaMda
vaiShNavaagamabharita vaiShNava priya
vaiShNava kamala sUrya vaiShNava kumuda caMdra
vaiShNava kulOtkRuShTa viShNuparaneMdu ||2||

hariyalladanyatra ariyada mahaamahima
haripaadayugaLa sarasIruha bhRuMga
gurupuraMdaraviThala paradaivaveMdaruhi
dhareyoLu sujanara porede guruvyaasamuni ||3||
