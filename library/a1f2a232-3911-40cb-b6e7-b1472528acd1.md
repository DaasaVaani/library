ditijarigeduraaMta kRutaaMta
gati nI namage guNavaMta hanumaMta || pa ||

kEsaritanaya dakShiNagaagi baMde 
varuShagaLiMdali balugiriyanu taMde
Isha raghupati sEve ghanavaagi niMde
asura raavaNana sarva sainyava koMde || 1 ||

aMbudhiya daaMTi sIteya rUpa kaMDe
kubuddhi raavaNana purava sUregoMDe
vibudhara snEhava maaDide balugaMDe
prabuddharaMdadi puNyaPalarasa uMDe || 2 ||

hayavadanana kRupe priya hUDi potte
apriyavaada bhavataruvina bEra kitte
bhayava KaMDisi namagabhayavanitte
jayajaya praaNanaatha namO namaste || 3 ||
