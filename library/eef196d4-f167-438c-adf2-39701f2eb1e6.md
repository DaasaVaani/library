siriramaNa tava caraNa dorakuvudu hyAMginnu parama pApiShTha nAnu ||pa||
narahariye ninna nAmasmaraNe mADade | narakakke guriyAdenO | innu | ||apa||

kere baavi dEva maMdiragaLannu keDahi nA hiridAgi mane kaTTide |
nerenaDeva mArgadoLu aravaTTigegaLanu tharatharadi bicci tegede |
parama saMBramadiMda araLiya mara kaDisi koresi bAgila mADide |
varamaMdirava mugisi haruSha citrava baresi paripariya sauKya suride | merede ||1||

hosa maneya kaMDu balu hasidu BUsuraru bare kosari hAkuta dabbuta |
SaSimuKiyE bAreMdu asuvane baNNisuta hasanAgi avaLolisuta |
daSami EkAdaSi dvAdaSi dinatrayadi aSanaveraDu hottuNNu tA |
GasaGasane tAMbUla paSuvinaMdadi meluta kusumagaMdhiya ramisuta | satata ||2||

snAna saMdhyAna atimauna gAyatri japa BAnugarGyavanu koDade |
SrInivAsane ninna j~jAnapUrvaka pUje nAnoMdu kShaNa mADade |
byaane baMdaMtAge hIna vidhaveya kaiya nAnA vidhAnya tiMde- noMde |
dInatvadali dAna dharmavanu maaDade SvaananaMdadi cariside | baride ||3||

sAkalyadiMda sAligrAmakaBiShEka AkaLhaalali mADade |
nAlkeMTu nAyigaLa sAki maneyoLu baduka-bEkeMdu hAlu hoyde |
kAku buddhigaLiMda guDiguDi naSyapuDi hAki BaMgiya sEdide |
lOkaniMdakanAgi pAtakake kai hacci anEka jUjugaLADide | biDade ||4||

uttama brAhmaNara vRuttiyanu keDisi brahmahatyagAranu eniside |
matte muMji maduve samayakke  nA hOgi satta suddiya pELide |
vittaviddavara benhatti dUtara kaLuhi kuttigeyanu  koyiside |
hattu janarenheNake nitya kalhoDeyutire mRutyudEvare eniside | biDadE ||5||

bAdarAyaNa kRuta BAgavata kELuvalli Adaravu puTTalilla |
vAdigaLa matavaLidu madhvasiddhAMtada hAdige hOgalilla |
SOdhisida cinnakke samanAda vaiShNavara pAdakke bILalilla |
vEda bAhyarigeragi apasavyamananAgi OdikoMDeno idella | salla ||6||

kShitiyoLage innAru hitava bayasuvarenage gatiyEnu pELo konege |
satata tava dhyAnadalli ratanAgi iruva sanmatiya pAlisayya enage |
patita pAvananeMba birudu avaniya mEle SRuti sArutideyO hIge |
SitikaMThanuta jagannAthaviThala ninage nutisi morehokke hariyE | doreyE ||7||
