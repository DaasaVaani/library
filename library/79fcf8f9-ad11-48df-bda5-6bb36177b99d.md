karuNisi vRuShThigareyo kamalanayana
nararugaLa aparaadha nODadale bEgadalli ||pa||

annadiMdali bhUtagaNa udbhavisuvudu
annadiMdali abhivRuddhiyaaguhudu
annadiMdali trividha saadhanavu pUrtipadu
parjanyadiMdali anna janisuva kaaraNa ||1||

bhIShaasmaadvaataH pavate eMbOktiyali
I samastamararu ninna bhayadi
byaasarade tamma tamma vyaapaaragaLa maaDi
pOShisuvaru jagava prItiyiMda ||2||

varShaM nigRuhNaami sRujaami ca eMdu
hariye ninniMdale vyaapaaravaaytu
surapati varuNaadi suraru nimittaru
paramaguNaasaaMdra hE karuNaabdhicaMdra ||3||

naranobba prabhu tanna paricararanu
parama abhimaanadali paripaalipa
suraru brahmaadigaLa doreye ninagI maatu
sarihOdarI vacana salisu kRupeyiMda ||4||

avaavasaadhanadi aavasukhavaidutire
dEva ninnayakIrti baruvadeMtO
dEvadEvESa guru vijayaviThThalarEya
saavadhaanadi tiLidu paalipudu binnapava ||5||
