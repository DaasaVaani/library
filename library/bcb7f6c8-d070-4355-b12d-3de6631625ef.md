vidurana bhaagyavidu |I
padumajaaMDa taledUgutalidako ||pa||

kururaayanu KaLananujanu ravijanu |
gurugaaMgEyaru nODutire ||
harisi rathava naDesuta hari bIdili |
barutire janarige sOjigavenisuta ||1||

hRudayadoLage nelegoMDiha hariyanu |
edurali kaaNuta viduranu taa ||
sadanava nUkutalaakShaNa bIdige |
mudadiMdali kuNidaaDuta baMdanu ||2||

dUradaliha shrIshana nODuta bahu |
haaruta cIruta maimaredu ||
vaaridhaareyanu nEtradi surisuta |
baari baari balu higguva manadali ||3||

aDigaDige tanugeDavutalELuta |
dRuDhabhakutili gadgada svaradiM ||
nuDigaLu todaluta rOma puLakitadi |
duDu duDu ODaaDuva dashadisheyali ||4||

haaDuva haaruva cIruvudellanu |
nODi nagutalire maadhavanu ||
nIDuta karava piDidu bigidappi koM |
DaaDuta bahaLa yamaaMshananu ||5||

ollanu kujanara sEveyeMba nuDi |
sallisuvudakaa samayadali |
malla viduranaa kutsita kuTIra |
dalli baMdu kuLitanu idE saakShiyu ||6||

shrInivaasa maMdiradoLu bara |
laanaMdaabdhiyoLIsutale ||
EneMbenu sukRutavEnodagito |
taane baMda bahu vicitravenutali ||7||

gaMgOdakadali padayugmava toLe |
daMgake pUsi sugaMdhavanu ||
maMgaLakara puShpaMgaLa samarpisi |
kaMgaLa daNidaNi nODuta mUrtiya ||8||

vaaridhi maneyaadaKilEshage gO |
kShIra svalpa koDalaadaradi ||
vaarijaakSha karasaMpuTadi kuDidu |
nIraMdadi harisida bIdiyoLage ||9||

oMdu kuDute paalige haLLaayitu |
maMdaradharana mahaatmeyanu ||
iMdiregasadaLa ciMtisaleMdigu |
maMdanu ballane eMdu pogaLutiha ||10||

suraraakaashadi neredu nODi balu |
haruShadi vidurana pogaLuvaru ||
siridEviyarasa puraMdaraviThalana |
sharaNare dhanyaru dhanyaru dhareyoLu ||11||
