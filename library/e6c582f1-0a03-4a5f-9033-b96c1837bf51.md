parama dayaakari nI baare
varagaNapana janani cinnada manavittu
harige nIDa bEkamma ennamma  ||1||

raMgana nirmaalyadiMda pUjipene
tiMgaLu oMdu nI pati saha irabEku
aMgane vara gauri paarvati dEvi ||2||

baDavaroDeyana naMdi vaahanavEri
taDa maaDade baare muDi hUva nIDe
mRuDa sakha eMteMbe puraMdara viThalana
aDiya sEveya nIDi nI daya maaDe ||3||
