```source-latin
kaMDe kaMDe svAmiya bEDikoMDe ||pa||

kaMDe tirupati veMkaTESana kAruNyAtmaka sArvaBaumana 
kAmitArthagaLIva dEvana karuNAnidhi eMdenisi merevana ||apa||
 
kOTisUrya prakASavenipa kirITavanu mastakadi kaMDenu 
nOTakAScaryavAda nagemuKa nosaloLage tirumaNiya kaMDenu ||
sATi illade SaMKa cakravu catura hastadaliralu kaMDenu 
bUTakada mAtalla kELirO BUri daivada ramaNanaMGriya ||1||
 
tappu kANike kappagaLa samasta dESagaLiMda tarisuva 
uppu OgaragaLane mADisi ucitadiMdali haNa gaLisuva, 
ippattu duDDige sEru tIrthava oppadali kraya mADikoDisuva 
sarpaSayana SrI SESha giriya timmappa veMkaTaramaNanaMGriya ||2||
 
uradi SrIdEvi iralu kaMDenu, unnatada kaustuBava kaMDenu, 
garuDa gaMdharva kinnarAdigaLiralu eDabaladalli kaMDenu ||
tarataradi Baktarige varagaLa karedu koDuvuda nAnu kaMDenu, 
Saradhi Sayana SrI SEShagiriya, varada puraMdara viThalanaMGriya ||3||
```
