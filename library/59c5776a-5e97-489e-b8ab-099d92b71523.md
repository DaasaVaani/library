bhaktajana paalaka bhaktisukhadaayaka
muktISa dInabaMdhu kRuShNa ||pa||
sRuShTiyoLu ninnaMtha dEvaranu naa kaaNe
satyavati sutane kaayO kRuShNa ||apa||

AnaMda tIrtha muniya dhyaanipara saMga 
AnaMdadali nillisO kRuShNa
dInajana maMdaara nIneMdu naMbideno
saanuraagadali kaayO kRuShNa ||1||

keTTa janara saMga iShTu dinavU maaDi
bhraShTanaagi pOdenO
beTTadoDeyane ninna muTTi bhajisuva bhaagya
iShTagaLa enage koDisO kRuShNa ||2||

ajajanaka gajavarada bhujagaSayanane ninna
bhajipa bhaagyavane koDiso kRuShNa
nijavaagi ninhoratu salahuvara naa kaaNe
vijayaviThThalarEya kRuShNa ||3||
