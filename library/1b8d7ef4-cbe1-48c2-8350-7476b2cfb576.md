ninhoratu porevavara naanariye hariye ||pa||

mEdinipatiyE ninnaaraadhane maaDisO hariyE ||1||

dhyaana maaDuve maana abhimaana

paMkajaakSha mukuMda maadhava 
kiMkaraamaravaMdya SrIhari
saMkaTagaLanu nI biMkadiM paripaalipa
kaMkaNa dharisida SrIveMkaTaviThThala hariyE ||3||
