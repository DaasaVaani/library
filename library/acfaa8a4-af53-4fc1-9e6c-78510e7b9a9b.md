narasiMha maMtra oMdiralu saaku
durita kOTiya taridu bhaagyavanu koDuva ||pa||

shishuvaada prahlaadana baadhe biDisida maMtra
asura kuladavarige shatru maMtra |
vasudheyoLu paatakigaLaghava hIruva maMtra
pashupatige priyavaada divya maMtra ||1||

diTTa dhRruvaraayanige paTTagaTTida maMtra
shiShTa vibhIShaNana poreda maMtra
kaTTakaDeyali ajaamiLana salahida maMtra
muTTi bhajisidavarige mOkSha maMtra ||2||

hiMDu bhUtava kaDidu tuMDu maaDuva maMtra
koMDaaDO lOkake pracaMDa maMtra
gaMDugali kadana uddaMDa vikrama maMtra
puMDarIkaakSha puraMdara viThala maMtra ||3||
