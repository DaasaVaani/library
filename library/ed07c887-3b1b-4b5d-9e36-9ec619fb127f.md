```source-latin
SrImadaacaaryara mata bhramara naa ||pa||
sumaastranayya paraaga hIruta
ramisutihe nI bhuvanadoLu  ||apa||

baMDuNiya tera naama uMDu
aMDaleve bommaaMDadoLage
kaMDoDane kaimugidajaaMDada
pracaMDadEvana toMDanenisuva ||1||

pakkakiruvaa cikka krimigaLu
cItkarisi kUguvuda lekkise
cokka gaMdha rasaanubhava sukha
dakkadE nI rakkasaravaayge  ||2||

taratarada parimaLada hUbana
tirutirugi rasa hIrutali Gana
paripari paraatparavanuMDu
hirihiri higgutali carisuve ||3||

araLadale baaDuva hU hUvE
maruLa maatina tiruLalEnide
saara Saastra paraaga tuMbiha
maruta makaraMdakadu samave ||4||

madhuva hIrida madadalirade
saadhujana saMgadali badukuve
padumanaabha prasanna veMkaTana
aDigaLali huDiyaagi nalive  ||5||
```
