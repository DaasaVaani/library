matte kShIraabdhiyoLu malagidaMdu ninna
truvvi truvviyeMdu tUgidaraarayya
A jaladhiya darbhaSayanadi ninnanu
jO jO jO eMdu paaDidaraarai
A rame SrutigUDi pogaLuvaMte
naarada mahatiya mITuvaMte sa-
mIraja svaravetti paaDuvaMte
SrIramaNa ninna pogaLaballene naanu
kaNNumucci nagutiMtenna satige
kaNNu mucci kRuShNa kiviyaaMtidda ||1||

ninnurada mOhadarasiya cenna celuvu sarasagaLiMda
ninna pokkuLa brahmana naalku vadanada vEda GOShaNagaLiMda
ninnudarada jagaduluhiniMda ninna bhaktaru kareva cIraaTadiMda
innu nidre baarade iMteMba gOpidEviya maatige
kRuShNa naaci kaNNumuccida ||2||

SubhaguNaMgaLa KaNiye jO jO
bhaktara ciMtaamaNiya jO jO
muni hRudayaaMbuja haMsane jO jO
sarvadEvOttaMsane jO jO
mukuti kaaraNa puNyanaamane jO jO
SrIkRuShNa vaikuMThadhaamane jO jO ||3||

lIla mUlakaaraNa laali baalalIlaa laali
mUlOkadoDeya laali gOpaala baala SrIkRuShNane laali ||4||

parabommane enna tamma baareMba
ajana taMde enna kaMda appeMba
aruhinoDale enna mariye muddiyeMba
gOpI dEviya nOMpi taaneMtO
idake baMdappi muddIva naliva
muddu maiya kRuShNanadaye taaneMtO ||5||
