ninna pUje horatilla enna vyaapaaravella ||apa||

snaana saMdhyaavaMdana hOma mauna japa sadgraMtha vyaa-
khyaana yaj~jasaadhana saMpaadana adhyayana
naanaa rasa naivEdya bhOjana taaMbUla carvaNa
maanini modalaada syaMdana gaanada bhOgagaLella ||1||

sarvEMdriyagaLaliha Sarvaadi dEvarkaLige
sarvacEShTaapradanaada praaNage nirvaahakanaagi
sarvajIvara kaiyiMda sarvaj~jaana karmagaLanu
sarvadaa maaDisi avara sarvaSubhabhOktanahe ||2||

aMgaaMgagaLalli suraru kaMgaLalli bhaagyalakShmi
uMguShThadi lOkapaavane gaMgaadEviyu
hiMgade ninnalliralu tuMgaguNa paripUrNa
raMgaviThala neleyaagi maMgaLaaMgane mannisuveyo ||3||
