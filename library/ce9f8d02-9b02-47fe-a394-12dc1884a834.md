```source-latin
idane bEDuve raaGavEMdra gururaaya
sadamalaMtaHkaraNa dRuDhabhakuti j~jaana ||pa||

ninna nijadaasara daasyavanu koDabEku
nannadeMbahaMkaaravane biDisabEku 
ninna sEveyu bEku anyar^haMbala bEDa
ninna raghupatiya saMtata smaraNe bEku ||1||

ninna nija bhaktara sahavaasavirabEku
ninna dvEShigaLiMda dUrirasabEku
ninna hastOdakada amRutaannavannu uNisi
ninna paadOdakava nI kuDisabEku ||2||

madhvamata siddhaaMta manana maaDisabEku
shuddha taatparyada sudheyuNisabEku
kShudravaada vicaara durbuddhi baradaMte
tiddi bhUpativiThalana tOrabEku ||3||
```
