```source-latin
vaadiraaja nija mOda nIDuva 
tavapaada  tOrisayya hE jIyaa ||pa||

vEda vEdya yatyaaraadhaka sujanOddhaara 
maaDida paadavO gururaayaa
mEdini surarige saadaradiMda bOdha
nIDida paadavO mahaaraayaa ||1||

vaMdya satI janake saMdEha maaDeda
kaMdara koTTa paada yatiraayaa
maMdabhaagya bhaagya saMdOha dina
dinaa maMda nIDuva paadavO suranaatha ||2||

naShTa nayana mahaa kuShTa janara 
nija iShTaa nIDuvO mahaaraajaa 
kaShTa baDuva sujanaaniShTa kaLedu sarvO-
tkRuShTarenisida paadavO hE prabhuvE ||3||

ellara manOratha nillade sarvada 
sallisiruva paadavO karuNaaLO
phullalOcana ballida manOratha
sallisidEnO paada SrIkaraa ||4||

paataka parihaaranaatha rakShaka
bhaavi dhaataa ninnaya paadavO
hE taa taa daataa gurujagannaatha viThala
nija dUtana Subha paadavO mannaathaa ||5||
```
