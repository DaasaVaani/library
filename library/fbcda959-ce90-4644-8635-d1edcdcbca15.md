```source-latin
vijaya AriMdalenage Aguvudo
vijayadaasara karuNadiMda baruvudu ||pa||

paratatva vicaara paramaniShTharasaMga
paripariya sukarma pariNaamavu
paravaadigaLa tuchCIkarisi avaruktigaLa
hariye sarvOttamaneMdaridu baaLuwaMtha ||1||

bhUsurarasEve yaatre daana dharma
graasa uNa uDuvOdu lEsu ella
vaasudEvana guNagaLaaseyu kELuta 
klESa taTTade harivupaasaneyaa maaLpaMtha ||2||

j~jaanigaLiM maanakShONiyoLage SaSyu
hIna janarige dainyaavanu baDade
naanaa pari kavanagaLu j~jaani sammata racisi
SrInivaasanna daasaanudaaseMbaMtha ||3||

baMdhu janasaMdaNi ceMduLLa AbharaNaa
naMdadi uDugore utsaahavu
iMdirESana stOtradiMdale dinavannu
oMdu gaLige mareyadaMdadi iruwaMtha ||4||

apaara januma ivara paada ASraisi
SrIpatiyadhyaana maaDida kaaraNa
gOpaalaviThalanna rUpadhyaanadi nilisi
I pariyali enna kaapaaDu eMteMba ||5||
```
