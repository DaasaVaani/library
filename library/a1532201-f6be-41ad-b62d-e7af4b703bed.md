jiShNusakhane kRuShNane 
kRuShNa kariya kaayda karuNi ||pa||
kRuShNa poreyo enna viShaya-
tRuShNeyiM saayale dammayya  ||apa||

hiMde maaDida karmadaDavi
yeMdige savaruveno gO-
viMda ninna karuNasudhe i-
nneMdu paDedu sukhipeno
iMdumaaDida aghagaLu balu 
daMdugakoLagaade nInu
kuMdu kujanadUra ninna 
kaMdaneMdu salaho taMde ||1||

kaamavenna kamalanaabha 
sImegaaNaliyyado ni-
ssIma mahima krOdha enna 
saamava naDugisutide
bhramitanaade balu
prEmava tappisi suraktaanu hE daiva enna
aa mahaabhayava nivaariso    ||2||

sukhaduHkha jIvanakke 
kakku bakku bhayada bEsa-
rakke biDade ninna 
lekka maaDaliyyado
chikkatanadi 
sikki durviShayadi noMde mai-
yikkadaMte hayavadana 
dakkisenna anudina   ||3||
