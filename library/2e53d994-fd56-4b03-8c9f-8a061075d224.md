iMdriyagaLa sukhava neccadiru kaMDya
iMdraadigaLa bavaNeyannu nODikoMDya
eMdeMdigilli dEhava kaLeve kaMDya
hiMdumuMdina gatiya tiLiyadava bhaMDa ||3||

tilakOrdhwa puMDragaLa haccalu bEku
naLinanaabhana smaraNeyoLu muLugabEku
