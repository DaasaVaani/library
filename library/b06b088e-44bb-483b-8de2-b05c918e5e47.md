```source-latin
Hari dinadali unda nararige ghora 
naraka thappadu endu shruthiyu saaruthalide ||pa||

Gova konda paapa saavira viprara 
jeevahathyava maadida paapavu
Bhaavajanayyana dinadalundavarige 
keevinolage haaki kudisuva yamanu ||1||

Ondondu agalige koti koti krimi 
andina annavu naayimaamsavu
Mandaradharana dinadalundavaranu 
handiya suduvante sudisuva yamanu ||2||

Anna udaka thaamboola darpanagalu 
chinna vasthragalella varjithavu
Thanna sathiya sanga maaduva 
manujana bennali karula thegisuva yamanu ||3||

Jaavada jaagara krathu naalku saavira 
jaavanaalkara phalake mithiyillavu
Devadevana dinadi nidreyagaidare 
huri-gaavalinolu haaki hurisuva yamanu ||4||

Inthu ekaadashi upavaasa jaagara 
santhatha ksheeraabdhishayanana pooje
Santhoshadindali maadida janariga-
nanthaphalavaneeva Purandaravithala ||5||
```
