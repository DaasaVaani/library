summane baruvude mukti guru-
dharmabhikShava bEDidavage sukIrti || pa ||

manadalli dRuDhavirabEku duShTa-
janasaMsargagaLanu biDalibEku
anumaanavanu suDabEku tanna 
tanumanadhanavanoppisikoDabEku || 1 ||

kaamakrOdhava biDabEku 
harinaama saMkIrtaneyanu maaDabEku
hEmadaaseya biDabEku 
harinaamave gatiyeMdu nere naMbabEku || 2 ||

vyaapaara varjisabEku j~jaana-
dIpada kaaMtiyalyOlaaDabEku
paaparahitanaagabEku namma 
gOpaalaviThalanna nerenaMbabEku || 3 ||
