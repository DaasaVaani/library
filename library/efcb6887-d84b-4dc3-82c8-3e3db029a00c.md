```source-latin
dEvi aMbujavalli ramaNane 
bhUvaraaha dayaanidhe ||pa||
pavamaanana divyakaradali 
sEve saMtatagoLLuve ||apa||

avaniyoLu SrI muShNa kShEtradi 
nI vihaarava maaDuve
bhava vimOcana bhaktavatsalaa 
kavigaLige karuNaakara ||1||

atyagaadha sushIla jaanhavi
suttu ShODaSatIrthadi
nitya puShkaraNiya garbhadi
nitya iruvudu kaMDe naa ||2||

mattu baNNipe tIrtha taTadali
uttamaagnEyabhaagadi
cittavEdyadi kalpataru
aSvattha rUpadi iruvanaa ||3||

karagaLeraDanu kaTiyaliTTu
kOrehallane tOrutta
dharisi cinmaya saaligraamada
saravu I pari beLagutaa ||4||

Ganna SvEtaVvaraahaa mUruti
enna pUrvada puNyadi
ninna Subhakara pAdapaMkaja
vannu kaMDenu iMdu naa ||5||

suMdaraanana kaMja madhupanaa
iMdu nODida kaaraNa
baMda duritagaLella pOdavu 
caMda shubhakaravaadawu ||6||

mallamardana vaikuMThadiMda
mella mellane baMdeyaa
JallikaavaNadalli kuLitu
ella bhaktara salahuve ||7||

sUkaraasyane ninna paadaka
nEka vaMdane maaDuve
SOkahara gOpaalaviThThala
nI karuNisI rakShiso ||8||
```
