```source-latin
karevarellarU kUgi karigoraLa ninage baagi
barabEkO shrIhariya carite aruhalu manige ||pa||

aMjikillade naMjanuMDeyo nI jagada |
aMjikeya kaLedeyo keMjaDeya shivanE ||
praaMjvalatapagaidu naaltaMje kalpagaLu |
prabhaMjanagatiraMcita shiShyanenisid~haranE ||1||

raamanaamada amalu bhUmiparigErisi |
nEmanityava tiLisidamaraadi suravaMdya ||
bhaame umegatiprEmadali hari mahime |
dhummagaiside ummaLada bhaava kusumapallavisi ||2||

baaMboLeya pettavana iMbiTTupoMdi nI |
kuMbhiNiyali aMbujanaabhage naMbugeyaade ||
shaMbhu gajacarmaaMbaradharane shaMkarane |
shaMbhO shivashaMbhO eMdu padaaMbujakeragi ||3||

mUlOka paavana jalava taleyalli |
tilaka PaNi PaalacaMdranihanalli ||
nIlagrIva kapaala piDidu karadali |
nilladaleyuta raamajapa maaLpa trishUli ||4||

uttareya kuvaranige uttarOttara mukuti |
pathava tOrida pramatha muni mahaadEvanE ||
cittadollabha namma siri prasanveMkaTana |
citta nammatta harivaMte kRupedOralu ||5||
```
