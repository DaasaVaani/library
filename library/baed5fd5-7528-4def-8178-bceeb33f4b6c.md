jaya vAyu hanumaMta jaya BIma balavaMta | pa |

jaya pUrNa mativaMta jaya salaho saMta | apa |

aMjaneyali janisi aMdu rAmara sEve |
naMdadiMdali mADi kapi balava kUDi ||
siMdhu laMGisi KaLara vanava  BaMgisi |
sIteguMgurava koTTe laMkApurava suTTe || 1 ||

harige cUDAmaNiyanittu harigaLa kUDi |
Saradhiyanu kaTTi aribalava kuTTi||
uraga baMdhanadiMda kapivararu maimareye |
giriya saMjIvanava taMdu badukiside || 2 ||

dvAparAMtyadi pAMDu BUpanAtmajanenisi |
SrIpArthasArathiya Bajaka nInAde ||
pApi mAgadha bakara kIcaka hiDiMbakara |
kOpadiMdali taride mUjagadi merede || 3 ||

dhuradalli duryOdhanana balava nI taride |
aritu duSyAsanana oDala bagede ||
urava tappisi kauravana toDegaLa muride |
hariya kiMkara dhuraMdharagAru sariye || 4 ||

kaliyugadali kaLLarudisi durmatagaLanu |
balisi SrIhariya guNagaLanu maresi||
kaliyananusarisalu guruvAgi avatarisi|
KaLara durmata muride SrIkRuShNa paraneMde || 5 ||
