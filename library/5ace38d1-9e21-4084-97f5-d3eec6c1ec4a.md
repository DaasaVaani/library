```source-latin
Eke mamate koTTu daNisuvi raMga 
nI karuNadi enna paalisO kRuShNa ||pa||

ninna haMbalisade anya viShayagaLi-
gennanoppisuvudu nItavE
mannisi dayadi nI enna paalisalu naa
ninna nEmake pratikUlanE raMga ||1||

tanuvu tannadu alla tanu saMbaMdhigaLeMbo-
renagyaaro taanyaaro avarige
dhana modalaada IShaNatrayagaLanubhava hiM-
dina dEhadaMtallave raMga ||2||

iMdriyaMgaLu viShayadiMda tegeyalu gO-
viMda enna vaSake baaravo
iMdireyarasa suravRuMdavaMdita ninna 
baMdhaka shakutige namO namO ||3||

aritu aritu enagarelavavaadaru 
virakuti viShayadi baarado
karuNaasaagara nimma darushanavalladI
maruLu nIguva bagegaaNeno raMga ||4||

eMdige ninna cittake baruvudo enna
aMde uddharisayya karuNiye
suMdaravigraha gOpaalaviThala sukha
saaMdra bhava mOcaka namO namO ||5||
```
